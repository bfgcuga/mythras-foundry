Preserve each of the 57 individual status symbol illustrations, their meaning, colors, position and order, and the exact regular grid of 12 columns and 5 rows (last row has only 9 icons, last 3 cells empty black). Output a landscape image with 12:5 aspect ratio ideally 1728x720; every cell is an equal square. NO text, labels, borders, separators, checkerboards, or extra symbols. Make symbols clearly readable at tiny 24px size against black: preserve bright colored outlines, remove ambient background glow. For near-black symbols (dead skull, pressed person, prone person, invisible dashed person, restricted chains, suppressed person, shovel, black eye) lighten only their silhouette/outline to medium light gray so they remain legible on black, preserving their design. Do not reorder, remove or combine any icons. Essential: every cell background and edges pure black, no colored square backgrounds. Keep comfortable black margins around each symbol. This is a faithful background replacement and contrast cleanup, not a redesign.
